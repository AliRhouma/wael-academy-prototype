Feature Catalog كتالوغ الخصائص

Everything we could build next in the élève space to help Tunisian collège and lycée students prepare for exams, work through exercises more smoothly, study together, and follow a well-structured programme per subject — benchmarked against 40+ study apps and live-class ecosystems, grounded in learning science, and sized against the entities the prototype already holds. The full research with sources lives in docs/mobile/features-research.md.

How to read a card
EN name · التسمية بالدارجة · Nom français
The English working name, a suggested in-app label in Tunisian derja (RTL), and the French name used by teachers and parents.
Effort SML
S ≈ a few days to two weeks of front-end work on the prototype; M = two to six weeks or new content authoring; L = needs a backend, media pipeline or AI, or more than six weeks.
Impact ★☆☆★★☆★★★
★ nice to have · ★★ clearly moves exam results or retention · ★★★ core to the product promise (exam prep, exercises, shared study).
Horizon Quick winNextBig bet
Quick win = ≤ 2 weeks and reuses existing data · Next = 1–2 months · Big bet = needs backend/AI/media work.
Roles StudentParentTeacherAdmin
Who sees or operates the feature. Four roles are four filtered views over one dataset — never four apps.
Data it needs · NEW
Ties each feature to existing entities (years, subjects, chapters, lessons, sessions/recordings, live-sessions, exams, homeworks, quizzes, groups, users, resources, offers/subscriptions). Anything after NEW does not exist yet. “Reuses existing data” means no new content must be authored — at most a tag, a flag or a per-user list over existing collections.
Why it works · Seen in · Design note
The learning-science or motivation rationale (with the study behind it), the reference apps that do it well, and how it should show up in the UI in one line.

1 · Exam preparation التحضير للامتحانات

The Tunisian year is organised around devoirs de contrôle, devoirs de synthèse and the bac (session principale 3–10 June 2026; contrôle 29 June–2 July). Every card here turns those dates and the exam formats into retrieval practice — the two “high utility” techniques of Dunlosky et al. (2013): practice testing and distributed practice.

No exam-preparation cards match the current filters.

2 · Exercises & practice التمارين

Exercises are where the academy's teaching meets the student's hand. The cards move the student from a PDF série to guided attempts: progressive hints, worked solutions synced to steps, variants, interleaved sets, and a photo-to-correction loop with the teacher.

No practice cards match the current filters.

3 · Live & replays الحصص المباشرة والتسجيلات

Live séances and their recordings are the academy's core asset. These cards make a 90-minute recording usable for revision (markers, notes, checkpoints), make the live hour interactive (Q&A, polls, raise hand), and close the absence gap (attendance, catch-up).

No live/replay cards match the current filters.

4 · Shared & social نقراو مع بعضنا

Small, cooperative and opt-in. Pairs and groups rather than global rankings; effort rather than marks; a streak that can be shielded with an earned “Fire pass”. Patterns from Duolingo (Friend Streak +22% daily completion), Noon Academy, Forest, Focusmate and Habitica.

No shared/social cards match the current filters.

5 · Motivation & habits العادة والتحفيز

Daily goal, streak, focus timer, habit calendar, a few badges that certify competence, a weekly report for the student and a digest for the parent — with forgiveness built in (comeback mode) so one missed day never becomes a lost student.

No habit cards match the current filters.

6 · Organisation التنظيم

One planner that merges live sessions, homework deadlines, the official DC/DS windows and bac dates, with notifications that arrive at the student's own study hour, offline downloads, calendar sync and a quick-capture inbox.

No organisation cards match the current filters.

What each matière needs so that “the programme is good”: the content types, the exercise patterns that fit the discipline, the exam format (durations and coefficients per section, as tabulated by reviserbac.tn from the Arrêté of 24 April 2008 as amended — verify with the ministry), and what a complete chapter looks like. Methodology

Desk research on 26 October 2026: 52 distinct web searches and page fetches covering flashcard and retrieval apps (Quizlet, Anki, RemNote, Seneca, Kahoot!, Wayground), homework-help communities (Brainly, Knowunity, Vaia, Photomath, Gauth), mastery courseware (Khan Academy, BYJU'S), live-class ecosystems (Physics Wallah, Unacademy, Vedantu), French bac apps (SchoolMouv, Kartable, Les Bons Profs, Nomad Education, Studeo, digiSchool, Lumni), Tunisian and MENA players (TakiAcademy, devoir.tn, devoinet, reviserbac.tn, Najja7ni, Abwaab, Noon, Baims, Nafham, EdTrust), focus and habit tools (Forest, Flora, FLIP, Focusmate, Study Together, Habitica, Finch, Todoist), Duolingo's mechanics including the 2025 Energy change, Google Classroom, calendar subscription mechanics, and parent reporting (ClassDojo, Seesaw). Facts dated after 2024 were checked against at least one dated source; where sources conflicted the card says so or uses the more conservative figure. Impact and effort are the author's judgement against the prototype's current entities.

Each card reuses the prototype's vocabulary (دروس · تمرين · سلسلة تمارين · ملخص · كويز · فرض مراقبة · فرض تأليفي · الإصلاح) so labels can be pasted into /student-v2 as they are.
