## Behance & Dribbble: 3D mascots in education apps, 3D onboarding/splash, clay UI kits, isometric worlds, teacher characters, Arabic/MENA

### Takeaway

## Game UI galleries: title screens and main menus with hero characters, landscape mobile

### Takeaway
Supercell-style lobbies (Brawl Stars, Clash Royale) are the closest structural model for a landscape splash with a hero. A large character sits at the centre or toward one side, resources line the top edge, and a big pill CTA sits in the lower trailing corner. Game UI Database lets you filter title screens by Mobile and Landscape.

### Cited Findings
- **E18 · Game UI Database: Title Screen category** [snippet]. Defines a title screen as a small menu or button prompt next to the game's logo, with filters for Mobile and Landscape. **Borrow:** the minimal title-screen formula (logo, one prompt, hero art) for the splash. Keep it to one CTA. — [gameuidatabase.com](https://www.gameuidatabase.com/index.php?scrn=1)
- **E19 · Game UI Database: Title Screen, mobile filter set** [snippet]. **Borrow:** browse to compare how the logo and CTA are stacked over hero art across many mobile titles. — [gameuidatabase.com](https://gameuidatabase.com/index.php?set=1&vid=1&scrn=1)
- **E20 · Game UI Database: Mobile Controls** [snippet]. **Borrow:** thumb-zone placement for landscape (primary actions in the bottom corners). — [gameuidatabase.com](https://gameuidatabase.com/index.php?scrn=147&tag=2)
- **E21 · Interface In Game: Brawl Stars (Lobby, Menu, Select Brawler)** [snippet]. **Borrow:** the lobby composition. The search snippet does not describe layout; the details come from E22. — [interfaceingame.com](https://interfaceingame.com/games/brawl-stars/)
- **E22 · Brawl Stars main-menu guide (layout description)** [snippet]. The centre of the menu shows the selected Brawler with its rank, power level and trophy count, two "+" buttons sit either side of the Brawler model, and Settings is at the top. **Borrow:** the hero character dead centre, floating stat chips around it, and invite-style circular buttons flanking it. This maps directly onto Wael flanked by "+30 points" style chips. **Caveat:** busy, and a splash needs fewer elements. — [samurai-gamers.com](https://samurai-gamers.com/brawl-stars/main-menu-guide/)
- **E23 · Interface In Game: Clash Royale (main menu, player profile)** [snippet]. **Borrow:** a chunky bevelled button style with a darker bottom "lip" (this matches the project's existing `play` skin) and arena art as a framed stage behind the UI. — [interfaceingame.com](https://interfaceingame.com/games/clash-royale/)
- **E24 · UI/UX Brawl Stars main menu study, Víctor González Artigas (itch.io)** [snippet]. A fan redesign of the Brawl Stars main menu. **Borrow:** a designer's simplified version of a busy lobby, useful for seeing what can be removed. — [itch.io](https://thewolfg.itch.io/uiux-braw)

### Inferences
- Lobby screens consistently anchor the hero in the middle third and push UI to the edges. For the landscape splash, putting Wael to one side and the island to the other (with the logo and CTA in the remaining space) is an RTL-friendly adaptation of that pattern.
- The bevelled "lip" button in Supercell games is the same language as the project's existing gamified skin, so the CTA style needs no new invention.

## Websites with 3D hero characters and worlds (Awwwards, Godly, Spline, Rive): interaction ideas

### Takeaway
The best floating-world references are WebGL sites: Messenger by Abeto (a tiny planet with characters), Coastal World (an island) and Bruno Simon's portfolio (a drivable miniature world). For the character, cursor-following eyes and head turns are well-trodden and cheap to build in Spline (the Follow event with damping) or Rive (a hitbox plus a target constraint).

### Cited Findings

**Group E: floating miniature worlds (island composition, depth, lighting)**
- **E25 · Messenger, Abeto** [snippet, award confirmed]. A tiny 3D WebGL/Three.js planet on which you play a courier walking around to deliver mail. Won the Awwwards Developer Site of the Year 2025. **Borrow:** a small round world that floats in empty sky and fills about half the frame, with characters at tiny scale on it. This is a direct model for the floating town island. **Caveat:** a full 3D build is heavy, so fake it with a pre-rendered island plus CSS parallax. — [Awwwards Sites of the Year](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_year/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Messenger_(video_game)); live site [memory]: https://messenger.abeto.co
- **E26 · Coastal World, Merci-Michel** [snippet, award confirmed]. An island-style WebGL world and Awwwards Site of the Month (Aug 2022). ### Inferences
- Floating worlds on award sites share a recipe: one isolated landmass, empty gradient sky around it, a soft key light from the top or one side with rim light on the opposite edge, and a contact shadow or cloud skirt underneath. This is cheaply reproducible as stacked PNG or WebP layers.
- Cursor-reactive eyes are a solved, low-cost pattern. On touch devices the reaction should be driven by device tilt or tap, or fall back to idle blinks, because Spline's View Reset and Rive's idle states assume no hover.

## Recurring patterns across the set (character size, depth layers, CTA framing, backgrounds, floating chips, lighting)

### Takeaway
Across game lobbies, mascot onboarding and 3D award sites, the recurring formula is: one dominant hero (character or world), three to five parallax depth layers, one chunky pill CTA in the thumb zone, a gradient sky with a radial glow behind the hero, and small floating reward chips orbiting the hero. Lighting is soft top or side light with a coloured rim and a soft contact shadow.

### Cited Findings
- Lobby pattern: the hero sits at the centre with stat chips and circular "+" buttons arranged around it. — [samurai-gamers Brawl Stars guide](https://samurai-gamers.com/brawl-stars/main-menu-guide/)
- Title-screen pattern: a small menu or single button prompt beside the logo. — [Game UI Database](https://www.gameuidatabase.com/index.php?scrn=1)
- Floating-world hybrid: a 3D island with CSS-only UI animations on top. — [Awwwards: Coastal World](https://www.awwwards.com/coastal-world-by-merci-michel-wins-site-of-the-month-august-2022.html)
- A tiny planet with small characters as the whole hero. — [Awwwards SOTY](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_year/)
- Character reaction uses damped follow plus a reset when the pointer leaves. — [Spline docs](https://docs.spline.design/doc/follow-event/doc5CVQiJtfM)
- Character reaction also uses UI-state-driven eyes and pose (the login-screen pattern). — [Rive blog](https://rive.app/blog/animated-login-screen-design)
- Mascots are designed as an expression set spanning onboarding through gamification. — [Behance: Talking Duo](https://www.behance.net/gallery/235742943/Talking-Duo-Vector-Mascots-for-Kids-Learning-App)

### Inferences
These are synthesised design rules of thumb. They are not measured from the sources.
- **Character size:** a landscape splash hero usually spans 60–85% of screen height, cropped at the waist or thigh, or full-body at about 70%. The world or island takes 35–50% of the width on the opposite side. For RTL, put the logo and title on the right with Wael, and the island on the left or behind.
- **Depth layers (4–5):** (1) sky gradient with a radial glow; (2) distant clouds or a faint grid/pattern; (3) the floating island with its contact cloud underneath; (4) the character with a soft ground shadow; (5) foreground UI: logo, floating chips, CTA, plus optional foreground bokeh or particles. Parallax strength should increase toward the front (about 2 px for layer 1 up to about 12 px for layer 4 on pointer or tilt).
- **CTA framing:** one large pill with a bevelled lip, in lime on navy for the "one lime moment" (consistent with the project's `play` skin). It goes bottom-centre or in the bottom trailing corner, with a secondary ghost link ("J'ai déjà un compte" or its Arabic equivalent) beneath it. The character's gaze or gesture points toward the CTA.
- **Backgrounds:** a vertical teal-to-navy gradient, a lighter radial glow behind the hero (teal/lime at low opacity), and optionally a faint perspective grid or soft cloud band to sell the "floating" effect. Avoid busy patterns behind the character.
- **Floating UI chips:** two to four small rounded chips (for example "+30 XP", a streak flame, a star or badge), slightly rotated, with a gentle float (2–4 s, offset phases). Place them around the hero's shoulder line and the island, never over the face or the CTA.
- **Lighting:** a warm key light from upper-front or the side, a cool teal rim from behind (it separates the character from a navy sky), and soft ambient occlusion. Use the same light direction on the character and the island so the composite reads as one scene. That consistency is the single most common failure in 2.5D composites.
- **Restraint:** game lobbies (E22, E23) are busier than a splash should be. Borrow their chip and button language, not their density.

### Gaps
- No source gave quantitative norms (character height percentage, layer counts). The numbers above are design inference and should be validated against the actual images once they are opened by hand.
- No source documented how the CTA is framed specifically on education-app 3D splash screens.
